General Description

Geomarketia is a web-based platform that integrates Geographic Information System (GIS) technology to help business owners, investors, and local governments analyze market potential spatially. The platform provides interactive map visualization, clustering analysis, and AI-powered insights to support more accurate, data-driven business location decisions.

Conventional and intuitive approaches to selecting business locations lead to a high risk of business failure. Geomarketia addresses this by integrating spatial data, clustering analysis, and artificial intelligence into a single responsive and user-friendly platform, serving three main sectors: Retail, Food & Beverage (F&B), and Healthcare.

  • Map Analysis — Market distribution map with business location markers, radius selector (1.5–10 km), business detail popups, and an AI Chat for map-based geospatial analysis.
  • Cluster Area — Business density cluster visualization with area summary (density level, total businesses, average rating, total reviews, digital presence, dominant category, rating distribution, top businesses) and color-coded density markers.
  • Intelligent System — Interactive map for location recommendations, filterable by Sub District, Sub Category, and Top Rank.
  • Project Management — Browsable list of market analysis projects with map and cluster preview, pre-purchase AI Chat consultation, and integrated payment via Midtrans.
  • Admin & Manager Dashboard — Project management restricted to Manager, transaction and payment monitoring, sales report export to Excel, user data management, and role management handled by Admin.

User Roles & Access Rights

Manager
Manager — Project Control

The Manager is the only role authorized to manage the market analysis projects offered on the platform, ensuring project data stays accurate and up to date.

Access Rights

  • Add new market analysis projects along with their complete information.
  • Edit existing project details and datasets.
  • Delete projects that are no longer relevant or accurate.
Admin
Admin — Monitoring & User Management

The Admin oversees platform transactions and manages user accounts and roles, without direct control over project content.

Access Rights

  • Monitor purchase transactions and view payment details.
  • Export sales reports to Excel (.xlsx) format.
  • View, edit, and delete registered user data.
  • Promote or demote user roles between Manager and User.
User
End User — Limited Access

Users are business owners, investors, or analysts who browse and purchase geospatial project datasets to perform location-based market analysis.

Access Rights

  • Browse & Search: explore available projects, filter by category, and preview project details with map and cluster previews.
  • AI Chat: ask market analysis questions before and after purchasing a project.
  • Purchase & Pay: buy project datasets and complete payment through Midtrans.
  • Map Analysis: interactive map with business markers, radius selector (1.5–10 km), and business info popups.
  • Cluster Area: density heatmaps and clustering statistics for deeper market insights.
  • Intelligent System: location recommendations filtered by Sub District, Sub Category, and Top Rank.
D

Development Method

Geomarketia was developed using the Agile Software Development method, which emphasizes an iterative, flexible, and collaborative approach to responding to dynamically changing system requirements.

  • Requirements & Planning — Identified the platform's functional needs, such as spatial data integration and map visualization features, based on priority.
  • Analysis & Design — Translated requirements into system architecture, GeoJSON data structures, and interface prototypes built in Figma.
  • Iteration / Development — Modular coding cycles using Next.js and mapping libraries to dynamically present location data.
  • Testing & Quality Assurance — Black-box testing to validate coordinate accuracy and platform responsiveness.
  • Deployment — Migrated the code to a hosting server so Geomarketia can be accessed publicly.
  • Review — Evaluated system performance and spatial data accuracy to ensure the results align with the functional objectives and user needs.
