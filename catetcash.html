About

CatetCash started from a simple frustration — most budgeting apps are too complex and cluttered. Built for people who just want to know where their money goes.

A lightweight web application to track daily spending, stay within budget, and export reports when needed. No hidden costs, no credit card required, free forever.

Features

  • Record Expenses — Log any expense in seconds with description, amount, and category.
  • Edit Anytime — Made a mistake? Edit or delete any record at any time.
  • Monthly Budget — Set your monthly budget and see remaining balance in real-time.
  • Calendar View — Browse expenses by date using an interactive calendar.
  • Export to Excel — Download your monthly report as an Excel file, ready to share or save.
  • Authentication — Secure register and login system.
