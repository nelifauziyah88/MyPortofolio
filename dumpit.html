Key Features

  • Photo Upload: upload your own photos directly into beautiful ready-made templates.
  • Share via Link: generate a shareable URL to send your scrapbook to anyone, instantly.
  • Flipbook Style: personalized flipbook-style scrapbooks that feel warm and handcrafted.
  • Curated Themes: multiple thoughtfully designed themes and layouts to match your mood.
  • Privacy First: your data stays yours — no tracking, no selling, no compromise.
