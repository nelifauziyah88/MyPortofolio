A

General Description

Venus Cars is an elegant and trustworthy car buying and selling platform, specifically designed to deliver the best experience for both buyers and sellers. With a modern and easy-to-use interface, the application makes it easy for users to find their dream car or sell their vehicle quickly, safely, and conveniently.

A wide range of car categories is available, including SUV, MPV, Sedan, and Sport, as well as popular models such as Honda Mobilio, HR-V, Civic Type R, and New Honda City.

The system supports two primary user roles, namely Seller and Buyer, with the following workflow:

  • App Access — Displays the main page, car categories, and a list of cars for sale.
  • Search & Filter — Users can browse by category or type a car name such as "HR-V" or "New Honda City".
  • View Car Details — Users can view complete information including images, price, and description.
  • Register / Login — To buy or sell a car, users must register or log in first.
  • Contact Seller — After logging in, buyers can directly contact the seller or dealer via WhatsApp.
  • Complete Transaction — Transactions can be made in cash or on credit, subject to agreement between buyer and seller.

C

System Modeling

The use case diagram illustrates the interaction flow between the two main actors, namely Buyer and Seller, with the VenusCars system.

Seller
Admin — Full Access

The seller is responsible for managing product data and car images, verifying orders and payments, and monitoring sales performance.

Access Rights

  • Manage product data and car images (exterior & interior).
  • View and verify incoming orders from buyers.
  • Verify cash payments, down payments, and installments.
  • Manage car brochures and access the sales dashboard.
  • Analyze sales performance based on statistical data.
Buyer
End User — Limited Access

Buyers can search and view car information, register and log in, and manage the purchase process through to payment.

Access Rights

  • Search & Filter: search products by brand, model, specifications, year, category, and price.
  • Wishlist: save and manage a list of favorite cars.
  • Purchase: submit a cash or credit purchase and view payment simulations.
  • Payment: pay down payment, installments, or full cash and download a digital invoice.
  • Contact Seller: reach the seller directly via WhatsApp.
